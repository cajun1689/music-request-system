DJ Bridge 1.14.3 completes the club show controls and startup fixes.

- Start Club Night: 30 seconds of movers only in white with phased slow pulses and wide slow movement, followed automatically by normal club lights, colors and rotation. The updated native controller holds every non-mover master at zero during the intro, including playback auto-start cases.
- Stop Show: all lights down and automation stopped. White Only: steady white at normal levels, movement and pulses off.
- MA discovery uses the club network correctly on Macs with virtual adapters. The matching booth controller ignores reply addresses outside the Gaslight lighting LAN.
- Fixes the destroyed-window crash on reopening during shutdown. Operator action failures remain visible through background status updates.
- Includes a printable one-page visual club-controls guide.
- Includes the stable macOS network identity and universal Apple Silicon / Intel receiver.

The updated native controller is installed in the Gaslight booth show. App updates do not replace a show, change fixture patch, or raise lights automatically.
