DJ Bridge 1.14.1 adds Stop Show and White Only, fixes reopening a closed window during shutdown, and gives Bridge its own stable macOS local-network identity.

- Stop Show brings all lights down and stops movement, dimmer effects and automation.
- White Only restores normal light levels in steady white with movement and pulses stopped.
- Both controls also work with music connectors off, using a short connection without starting music listeners.
- Start Club Night opens with 30 seconds of movers only: white, phased dimmer pulses and wide slow movement. Normal club lights, colors and rotation follow automatically.
- The booth needs the updated DJ Bridge MA controller. Native Stop Show and White Only are also available on CLUB HOME and AUTO CONTROLS.
- macOS may ask to allow DJ Bridge to find devices on the local network. Allow this to connect to the booth.
